# Benefits Summary (fictional)

## Health plan
The company offers a standard health plan with medical, dental, and vision.
Enrollment happens during your first two weeks and during the annual open
enrollment window.

## Retirement
A retirement savings plan is available with a company match of up to 4% of
salary. You can adjust your contribution at any time through the benefits portal.

## PTO accrual
See the HR policy for PTO accrual details. Unused PTO rolls over up to a cap of
five days per year.
