# HR Policy (fictional)

## Paid time off
Full-time employees accrue 15 days of paid time off per year, plus 10 company
holidays. PTO requests go through your manager and should be submitted at least
two weeks in advance where possible.

## Leave of absence
Employees may request an unpaid leave of absence of up to 12 weeks for medical
or family reasons. Contact HR to start the process.

## Conduct and escalation
Workplace concerns should be raised with your manager first. If unresolved, HR
can be reached at hr@corp.local. All reports are handled confidentially.
