# IT FAQ (fictional)

## Password resets
To reset your domain password, visit the self-service portal at
http://portal.corp.local or call the IT help desk. Passwords expire every 90
days and must be at least 12 characters.

## VPN access
Remote access uses the company VPN. Request access through your manager; IT
provisions a profile within one business day.

## Printer access
Office printers are on the corp-net. If you can't print, confirm you're on the
corporate network and that the correct printer queue is selected.
