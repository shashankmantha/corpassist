# New Employee Onboarding (fictional)

## First day
Your manager will meet you to set up your workstation. IT will have created your
corp.local domain account ahead of time; pick up your laptop from the IT desk.

## Accounts and access
You'll receive access to email, the shared drive, and any team tools during your
first week. Access is role-based — request anything extra through your manager.

## First-week checklist
- Complete security awareness training
- Set up multi-factor authentication
- Review the HR policy and benefits summary
- Meet your team and manager one-on-one
